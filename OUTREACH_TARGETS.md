# Flow - Outreach Targets

## The pitch in one line
Every wallet below charges its users 0.25%-0.875% per swap. Flow charges ~0.04% and delivers a better net price. "Switch your router, your users pay 10-20x less and get better fills."

## Tier 1 - Best fit, start here

### 1. Rabby
- Why: DeFi-native user base that actually compares execution quality. Lowest existing fee (0.25%) means their users are price-sensitive and will notice better fills.
- Angle: "Your users are the ones who check prices. Give them provably better execution and a fee 6x lower than what you charge now."
- Send: ONE_PAGER_WALLET.md + fork test numbers (0.0401192 → 0.0401493 WETH on $100).

### 2. Coinbase Wallet
- Why: Base-native wallet on Base's own chain. Aligned incentives: better Base execution makes their ecosystem look good.
- Angle: "Built for Base, on Base. Your users get the best execution on your chain, and you can tell that story."
- Note: Slow-moving org, but the strategic fit is unmatched. Worth one well-crafted intro.

## Tier 2 - Strong fit

### 3. Rainbow
- Why: EVM-only, design-forward, 0.85% fee. Their brand is premium UX; better execution + lower fees is an easy story for them.
- Angle: "Premium feel should include premium prices. Cut your swap fee 20x and beat the market on every trade."

### 4. Bitget Wallet
- Why: 130+ chains, MPC security, aggressive on new integrations. Moves faster than US incumbents.
- Angle: "You list new networks first. Be the first wallet with auction-based execution on Base."

### 5. Phantom
- Why: Already routes Base swaps via their Crosschain Swapper. They feel the execution pain directly.
- Angle: "Your Base swaps can settle better than your current router gives you. One endpoint, no SDK."

## Tier 3 - High volume, harder to reach

### 6. Maestro (Telegram bot)
- Why: Multi-chain incl. Base, enormous swap volume, traders are fee-obsessed. Has a 25% referral commission program, they understand rev-share.
- Angle: "Your traders pay 1% per swap. Route Base flow through Flow and keep the spread as margin or pass savings to users."

### 7. Banana Gun (Telegram bot)
- Why: Multi-chain sniper bot, 1% fees on most chains, MEV-aware already. Speed-obsessed user base matches the auction model.
- Angle: "You already sell speed. Add best-price execution on Base underneath it."

- Any CEX wallet: compliance overhead kills the conversation before it starts.
