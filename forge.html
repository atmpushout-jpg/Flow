2 · Adjust your fighter

Start from a play style, then fine-tune with three plain sliders. Everything updates live — and every setting stays inside the Arena's fairness bounds, so it's impossible to build an illegal bot here.

💾 Data spending per match
Data is the fuel — every quote, verdict, and signal costs real cents, and champions win by spending less. The house fairness cap is $0.60 per match for every fighter, house cast included — this slider can't go past it.
🎯 How picky it is about entering
Low = jumps in early and often. High = waits, watches, and only strikes on the strongest signals.
🎲 How much it stakes per entry
The share of its cash it puts on each decision. Bigger stakes swing harder — both ways.
