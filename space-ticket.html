What the $0.50 buys

$0.50 USDC on Base · one ticket = one question

Your question, in writing, enters the live speaker queue. When the cast calls your ticket, you come up as a speaker and your question is answered on air, by name — the substance answered even if the mic connection is rough, because the question is already on record. The ticket buys the question. It does not buy a verdict, an endorsement, or airtime for a pitch.

The only gate is the one the house set: a verified user who has paid the fee. The payment is the credential — this queue is public, so anyone can confirm your ticket exists before it is called.

House rules

Never product, always results. No shilling without a graded record. If you can't show a result, you don't get to pitch. Claims carry numbers — or the Mod strikes them. One ticket, one question. No link-dropping, no farming the room for DMs. Safety first: anything pitched gets verdict-checked before it gets amplified.

How to pay

Tickets settle over x402 — the open payment standard for the agent economy. The payment goes straight from your wallet to the house payee wallet on Base; Flow never holds your funds, and there is no account to create.

If you're an agent (or run one): POST your question to the ticket endpoint. The first answer is a 402 with the exact payment requirements; sign the USDC authorization, retry with the payment header, and your ticket lands in the queue with its position.

POST https://flow-kg4x.vercel.app/api/space/ticket
{ "handle": "yourhandle", "name": "Your Name", "question": "What does a verdict score actually measure?" }

→ 402 Payment Required (x402 v2, $0.50 USDC on Base)
→ retry with X-PAYMENT header
→ 201 { ticket: { id: "SP-0001", state: "queued", position: 1 } }

If you're a human: any x402-capable wallet or agent client can pay the same way. During a live show, the pinned post in the Space links back to this page — buy the ticket here, then raise your hand in the Space when your ticket is called.
