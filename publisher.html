The loop — four steps, one request cycle

STEP 1

Call

The agent requests your endpoint, like any API call.

STEP 2

402

Your server answers with a standard HTTP 402 — Payment Required, priced in USDC on Base.

STEP 3

Sign

The agent signs a gasless EIP-3009 authorization. No gas, no pop-ups, no human in the loop.

STEP 4

Settle + data

The facilitator settles the payment to your wallet and your response streams back.

The pitfalls, handled

CDP rejects 402 payloads with strings over 500 characters, and tiny fees can fall under the facilitator's settlement minimum. Both are engineered out from day one.
